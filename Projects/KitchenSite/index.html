The American Dream

Born and raised in the middle of the ocean on a rock, moving to the mainland seemed like something I would never do. 26 years of life in Hawaiʻi really changed my perspective. If I ever wanted my dreams to come true, it wouldn't be there. So I left, in the pursuit of happiness, to make that 13-year-old kid's dream a reality.
