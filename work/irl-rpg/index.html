01 · The order

What it is.

The things a household has to do anyway, turned into a game. A daily quest board hands out quests across money, training, study, cooking and chores. Finishing them earns XP, levels, stat rewards and streaks for a character you build yourself.

It's made for the people in my house, and that's who is using it.

02 · Prep

One big app, then a family.

The first version did everything in one app. It has since been split into smaller apps that share one character and one backend.

  • Training Grounds covers training, learning paths, health tracking and flashcards. It absorbed two earlier apps, Health Quest and Library.
  • Budget reads bank notifications on Android to capture spending, imports CSV, and updates itself over the air from a server I host.

React 19 · TypeScript · Vite · Supabase (Postgres, RLS) · Capacitor · Electron · Kotlin plugins

03 · The cook

The hard parts.

  • Apps that share a record without wrecking it. Every app saves through versioned rows with optimistic concurrency, and round-trips the whole record, so one app never strips data another owns.
  • Access rules under test. The database's access rules are covered by contract tests.
  • Money is never created or lost. Each app has around 75 test files, and one of the checks is exactly that.
  • Real apps, not wrapped websites. The Android and Windows builds load their own packaged files instead of wrapping a hosted site.

04 · The guide

Nine creatures, one guide.

Training Grounds has a Mythic Guide: a companion you pick from nine creatures, name, and ask about today's training, an academy drill, or which quest fits your energy. Each creature has its own voice, and the guide's replies are shaped to match it.

An LLM companion is being built into the guide. Today it answers from a local rules engine, so nothing you tell it leaves the app.

  • Aster, the dragon: low, deliberate, old-fire counsel.
  • Ember, the phoenix: warm, rising, hopeful battle-muse.
  • Rook, the griffin: clear, noble, tactical watch-captain.
  • Mira, the kitsune: nimble, playful, clever side-path wisdom.
  • Sage, the owl: calm, precise, late-night scholar.
  • Noct, the raven: dry, quiet, streetwise omen-reader.
  • Vale, the stag: steady, patient, forest elder.
  • Solara, the unicorn: bright, gentle, reassuring oracle.
  • Koda, the wolf: grounded, direct, pack-guardian calm.
