# Portfolio

Taylar's portfolio, dressed as a working kitchen line: projects are orders moving from prep to the pass.

## Language

**Ticket machine**:
The kitchen order printer that is the site's main navigation; choosing a section prints a ticket for it.
_Avoid_: Ticket dispenser, take-a-number, nav bar

**Ticket**:
A paper slip standing for one project or section, printed by the ticket machine and hung on the rail.
_Avoid_: Chit, order slip, card

**Rail**:
The strip in the hero where tickets hang, one per project, showing each project's status at a glance.
_Avoid_: Order rail, status list

**Tier**:
One of the four groups projects are sorted into: on the pass, on the fire, test kitchen, or the prep list.
_Avoid_: Category, stage

**On the pass**:
The tier for released projects someone can use today.
_Avoid_: Done, shipped section

**On the fire**:
The tier for projects that run but are still being built.
_Avoid_: WIP, in progress section

**Test kitchen**:
The tier for stopped prototypes that led to the chef's special.
_Avoid_: Archive, retired, old projects

**Lineage**:
The ordered line of test-kitchen projects that led to the chef's special, each with what was carried forward from it.
_Avoid_: History, timeline, family tree

**Prep list**:
The tier for planned projects not started, or barely started.
_Avoid_: Backlog, roadmap

**Chef's special**:
The one featured project, given the most room on the page and a stamp on its rail ticket. Currently Multi-Apocalypse.
_Avoid_: Hero project, featured card, flagship

**Case study**:
A project's own page that goes deeper than its card on the home page.
_Avoid_: Detail page, project page, write-up
